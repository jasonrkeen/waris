The original source is not responsible for the sampling choices, object-count definitions, weights, interpretation or limitations of these derived tables. No provider endorsement is implied.

Transformation: ten consecutive cursor pages define the recorded frame; seeded without-replacement selection within first-observed completed aboveThreshold and priceQuotation domains; detail-time procedure/lifecycle and lot-linkage eligibility; tender-cluster weighting and coarse object-count aggregation. No attachments, individual identifiers or personal/party fields are redistributed. Procurement data is not treated as MIT- or CC-licensed merely because software or a registry website has that license.

The artifact-specific reuse assessment relies on the previously identified statutory source-attributed reuse basis for qualifying published open data in [Article 10-1, official State Tax Service reproduction](https://tax.gov.ua/dlya-gromadskosti/dostup-do-publichnoi-inform/normativn/53520.html), read with the [Ministry of Economy letter 3323-04/84579-06 of November 27, 2024, printed page 3](https://infobox.prozorro.org/upload/files/main/2276/667/list-3323-04-84579-06-vid-27112024.pdf). The letter discusses machine-readable procurement publication and further use, not an individual WARIS permission. The statutory mirror is dated 2025; a current consolidated legislative-original retrieval was unavailable. This review does not certify all subsequent amendments or grant blanket attachment/personal-data rights.

For these exact two coarse tables, the assessment combines that source/reuse evidence with verified unauthenticated public acquisition, source attribution, no personal/entity fields, no geographic or sector disclosure hierarchy, substantial tender-cluster denominators, and no entity-level accusation or adverse-action use. Public accessibility or the internal five-cluster support screen alone would not establish clearance. Neither does this assessment certify anonymity against every external linkage scenario. Raw records, membership and fine diagnostics remain excluded or held, and any expanded release needs individual review.

The procurement source field in each CSV preserves attribution when the CSV is used separately. Retain this document and the methods/limitations alongside any proposed release. The source records are mutable; do not present these results as current national statistics or a complete period.

## Selected Ukrstat metadata

Source: **State Statistics Service of Ukraine (Ukrstat)**, [portal](https://stat.gov.ua/en) and the exact official SDMX code-list routes/hashes in metadata_verification.csv. Revalidated October 20, 2026. The current portal states a default [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) basis; no contrary selected-code/table annotation was identified. Original code-list agencies are preserved: SSSU for regional-account indicators, KATOTTG and NACE_UA; SDMX for the annual-frequency code. Do not infer that Ukrstat owns all imported third-party material. No full SDMX codelist description or bulk codelist is reproduced here.

WARIS selected 64 code/name/description rows and added planning roles, recommended years, status notes and a dated verification table. Three proposed year rows are WARIS planning content. The historical shortlist itself is unchanged; the dated verification is new. These selections and interpretations are not official approval of indicators, temporal availability, sectors, benchmark integration or scoring.

The initial CL_FREQ request under agency SSSU returned HTTP 204. The retained DSD identified agency SDMX; its correct same-version route returned HTTP 200 and matched the selected annual code. The failed attempt is retained privately, rather than being replaced in the record. Successful current revalidation does not reconstruct the historical HTTP request or prove historical license equivalence.
