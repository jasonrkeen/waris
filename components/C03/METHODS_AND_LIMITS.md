# Frozen-study methods and interpretation

The recorded frame contains 1,000 unique IDs first observed across ten consecutive descending public API pages. API cursor ordering uses public_modified, not displayed dateModified. This bounded, mutable operational frame is not a national or complete reporting-period population. Other procedures, concealed/redacted records and records outside the traversal are outside scope.

The two predeclared feed-defined completed-procedure domains contained 28 aboveThreshold and 43 priceQuotation tenders. A seed generated before acquisition selected min(40,N) without replacement in each domain. The complete private selection and its probabilities were frozen before detail requests; there were no replacements, extra pages or outcome-driven extensions. Detail status controlled eligibility without changing sampling-domain membership. No selected method/status drift occurred in this snapshot.

Tenders are clusters, explicit lots are units, and no-explicit-lots tenders form a separate unit type. Bid IDs must be distinct and nonempty. Each explicit-lot bid needs unambiguous existing relatedLot links. Missing or malformed relationships make counts unknown, including on a tender with one explicit lot. Tender bid totals are never spread across lots. Award suppliers are not used as bidders. Completed parent/unit, non-test mode and unrestricted configuration are required for the primary measure.

The active-object measure requires active bid status and absent/active lotValue status. Treating absent lotValue status as no additional filter is an explicit assumption; it is not certification that an offer was legally valid. Known inactive states are retained in the source but do not enter the active-object numerator. Missing/unknown bid states make counts unknown. Empty bid arrays are observed zero, whereas unavailable fields remain unknown.

The observed table has 32 eligible lots in 28 aboveThreshold tenders and 40 eligible no-explicit-lots priceQuotation tenders. Exactly-one-active-object counts are 18 and 21, respectively. Visible-object counts yield the same numerators in this snapshot. These are object counts, not unique firms, winning suppliers, validated low competition or evidence of financial crime. No inactive linked objects or mixed-outcome parents were observed here; handling of those cases is tested behavior rather than demonstrated live-case validation.

The expansion weights are N/n: 28/28 and 43/40. Horvitz-Thompson totals condition on the recorded frame and detail-time eligibility. The weighted ratio uses the corresponding expanded numerator/denominator; common weights cancel within a domain. A ratio estimator is not itself unbiased. No confidence interval is presented. Tender clusters, not individual lots, determine the internal minimum support screen. Five is an engineering screen, not a legal anonymity rule or statistical calibration.

The priceQuotation estimate of 22.575 exactly-one-active-object units is not an observed fractional count or evidence about the three unsampled records. Its expanded eligible total of 43 is also an estimate. The aboveThreshold totals are a census only of the recorded-frame domain, not of Ukraine. Procedures and unit types must not be pooled, ranked as regions, or converted into an AML score. Both tables retain the scope and no-scoring labels in every row.

No parties, prices, procurement subjects, locations, buyer/firm breakdowns, record membership, rare regional cells or hierarchical totals are included. The two tables repeat the same procedure partition; the weighted table adds no new geographic or party-specific equation. External knowledge could still identify publicly available underlying tenders; this is not a claim of formal anonymization. The outputs add no individual allegation, private attribute or finding.

Raw acquisition, source hashes, exact selection and independent count checks are retained privately. The public material permits calculation checks but not independent recovery of the immutable historical input bytes. Public re-fetches are different vintages. These are exploratory descriptive research outputs, not empirical or operational validation of WARIS.
