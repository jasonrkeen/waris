# WARIS benchmark and merge-review memo — 20 October 2026

This is a new, locally prepared review memo, not a reconstruction of the historical memo or its generated timestamp. GRP remains benchmark-only. Unverified FIU claims are excluded. Nothing has been published.

The reviewed numerical comparison covers 25 WARIS regions in 2021 (24 oblasts plus Kyiv City). Official GRP values are in million UAH and are source-attributed in the previously cleared supplements. Synthetic prewar revenue indices are arbitrary, seeded first-party inputs, not measured revenue. National Ukraine is outside this 25-region comparison; historical source and territorial notes apply.

Official and synthetic values are independently min-max scaled across the selected 25 regions. The gap is official index minus synthetic index; the share ratio divides official GRP share by synthetic-index share. Neither calculation places measured GRP and measured revenue on a common economic scale. These are descriptive comparisons, not economic overstatement/understatement findings, causal evidence or AML predictive validation.

The legacy absolute-gap bins (75/50/25/10 points) and ratio cutoffs (2/1.25/0.50/0.80) reproduce the retained labels, but were not empirically calibrated. A small index gap does not validate alignment, and a large one does not establish economic error. For example, Zaporizhzhia has a share ratio near 0.993 while its independently scaled gap is about -69.65 points. The distinction follows from the two normalizations, not evidence of a measured economic discrepancy.

Use gap_summary.json and gap_review_reconciled.csv for exact arithmetic. Mean absolute gap is approximately 44.13 points, with the largest absolute gap at Cherkasy. These values are reproducible descriptions of the selected seed42 comparison only. They do not authorize replacing or rescaling risk fields.
