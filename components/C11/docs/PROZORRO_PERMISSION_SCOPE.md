# Prozorro permission scope and artifact decisions

Review date: October 18, 2026. A statutory reuse basis has been identified for qualifying public open data, with source attribution, using [Article 10-1 as reproduced by the State Tax Service](https://tax.gov.ua/dlya-gromadskosti/dostup-do-publichnoi-inform/normativn/53520.html). [Ministry of Economy letter 3323-04/84579-06, 27 November 2024, p. 3](https://infobox.prozorro.org/upload/files/main/2276/667/list-3323-04-84579-06-vid-27112024.pdf), published on Prozorro Infobox, discusses free further use of published open data in the procurement context. This establishes a more specific permission basis than software licensing or general public accessibility alone. It is not a named Creative Commons data-license finding or a blanket attachment/privacy clearance.

The official About page remains unavailable (HTTP 503). Its indexed statement on use/distribution, including commercial use, remains corroborative, not a retained full-page terms check. An unspecified license field in the OCP registry does not by itself cancel a statutory right; it also does not establish a named CC license. Software Apache licensing and the OCP site's own footer license are not applied to tender records.

Twelve archived WARIS procurement artifacts were inspected by schema and aggregate metadata. Six raw/list/detail/feature-row exports remain excluded, including record-linkable identifiers and embedded detail payloads. Two benchmark bridge/review files have transitive dependencies and cannot be cleared by Prozorro's permission basis alone. Four aggregate/summary outputs are candidates for individual review, with sample provenance, source attribution, privacy and coverage checks still required. No procurement dataset is bundled in the source candidate.

There are fewer than five tenders in 11 of 23 oblast aggregate rows and 46 of 56 oblast-sector aggregate rows. These counts are privacy/interpretation triage, not a legal threshold or proof of anonymity. The expanded row extract records UAH; this does not validate every future mixed-currency aggregation. Bounded API samples are not exhaustive procurement coverage or evidence of wrongdoing. War-related redactions further limit interpretation. See prozorro_artifact_scope.json.

The official initial text of [Law 4888-IX, 27 May 2026](https://data.rada.gov.ua/ogd/zak/perv/text/d555108.htm) contains delayed general implementation and exceptions in section XIV. It is recorded as a transition issue, not applied retrospectively to authenticate old extracts or to assert that all its rules were operational on the review date.

For any included research output, retain the exact public source/query, retrieval timestamp, input hash, sample and pagination/completeness information, source credit and transformations. Exclude unreviewed attachments and unnecessary personal/entity identifiers. Expanded features remain review-only and cannot alter WARIS scores under this work.
