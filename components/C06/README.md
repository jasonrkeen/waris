# WARIS historical GRP evidence and validation-scope supplement

Prepared locally on 20 October 2026; unpublished. This is a separately reviewed replacement component, not a silent change to frozen originals or rc11. The full research release remains blocked. GRP remains benchmark-only and outside production scoring and Monte Carlo. Sector allocation is unapproved; unverified FIU claims remain excluded.

The curated historical_GRP_unit_evidence_reconciled.csv has 488 numeric observations for 2004–2021. Every legacy row and its 17 fields was reconciled under the old label-as-code convention against both retained exports, and every value matched hash-verified official API responses. The replacement separates actual administrative identifiers from source labels and separates unit/scale/decimals codes from labels. National Ukraine is a separate overlapping aggregate and must never be summed with regions. The 28 source series comprise 25 WARIS regions plus Crimea, Sevastopol and national Ukraine; current codes/labels do not prove historical boundary equivalence.

Out of 504 possible region-year slots, 488 are numeric, 15 are explicitly not available, and Sevastopol 2014 is absent from the long export and API. The wide export has a blank cell at that location. No value, missing status or historical row was invented. series_unit_coverage.csv preserves the per-series distinction. Wide-series-level Not available notes affect 20 otherwise numeric 2004–2013 Crimea/Sevastopol cells when naively repeated across years; the corrected table preserves that wide export field separately and follows the matching long/API year-specific status. Source series and territorial notes, including the national series note, remain verbatim and are not extrapolated into an unverified year-specific territorial model.

The first-party validation_check_scope_review.csv explains all 22 legacy checks. The historical 21 pass/one review status pattern was reproduced after normal candidate preprocessing; statuses establish availability, structural facts or declared controls in their stated scope. They do not establish empirical AML validity, source-claim truth, operational effectiveness or publication readiness. Presence and no-scoring flags do not verify FIU or other held content. No unverified annual-report facts are reproduced here.

Three new figures show the official 2021 GRP benchmark (all 25 WARIS regions, billion UAH), source-presence states, and the scope of historical checks. Each has its exact plotted CSV beside it. They were generated with Matplotlib and visually inspected. Original charts are preserved privately and excluded as supplied from this component. No full WARIS PDF or dashboard rerun is claimed. The original rc11 generators are unchanged; controlled integration of the corrected outputs remains required before a full public application release.

Attribution and source fingerprints are mandatory companions; see ATTRIBUTION_AND_RIGHTS.md, the included receipt records and FILE_MANIFEST.json. Raw provider response bodies, original raw exports, private audit records, individual procurement data and unverified FIU content are not bundled. The curated table is a selected benchmark/unit-reconciliation output, not a redistribution of the original bulk Dataset Explorer exports.

Run method-boundary tests from tools with `python -m pytest test_reconcile_units.py -q`. Full historical reconciliation additionally requires the authorized retained exports/legacy evidence and the exact API responses located by the receipts, plus unchanged rc11 mapping code and pinned environment. Those raw inputs remain private under project policy; this ZIP alone does not recreate the original acquisition history. No newly retrieved response may replace a missing historical artifact without a separate, dated comparison.
