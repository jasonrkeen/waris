# Data, evidence and redistribution policy

This documentation revision retains the rc13 source/runtime baseline and bundles no supplied provider inputs, derived scores, charts or PDFs. It does not replace the user's full-research target or authorise release. Code ownership and MIT intent are user-confirmed; MIT does not relicense provider material.

The user confirms rotated credentials and a personal research/academic ACLED account. Neither rotation nor account history was authenticated. Known original values may be read privately for local comparison scans; never print, package or use them for authentication. The ACLED collector/cache and live entry point stay gated. Personal research does not automatically require a corporate/public-sector licence, but any external derivative use remains subject to applicable terms, transformations, attribution and disclosure review. Provider interpretations retain their recorded review dates; this documentation pass makes no new legal permission determination.

The retained ACLED bytes contain event dates from 2018–2019 and cannot establish later wartime coverage. Current code, source-date fields, recovery dates, matching hashes and successful arithmetic do not authenticate the historical request. No new download can supply a missing historical acquisition date. The separate inspector keeps absence/unknowns explicit, does not infer a complete frame and makes no score or rights decision.

All thirteen formerly held mixed artifacts are excluded unchanged from this release plan. Exclusion closes their pending disposition, not the underlying rights or scientific gap. The complete historical ledger has 23 narrowly eligible files with companion conditions and 152 exclusions. None of the 23 is automatically included here. Any future selected artifact needs an explicit inclusion decision, source chain, rights basis, privacy review, scope/attribution and reproducibility evidence. Restricted data may remain privately acquired by authorised researchers; no blanket redistribution prohibition is asserted for every provider.

GRP remains benchmark-only; expanded Prozorro review-only; verified FIU/SFMS national context-only; unverified FIU annual/mixed claims excluded; selected literature reference-only; operational controls documentation-only. Do not import historical output caches into a new public report. Earlier generated files are not retroactively repaired by current export gates.

Runtime data, outputs, environments, caches, secrets and provider responses stay outside the source ZIP and under the existing gitignore policy. Preserve provider-specific notices and dated source evidence. Public availability and hashing do not grant rights or prove acquisition. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md and release_specification.json for the remaining full-release gates.
