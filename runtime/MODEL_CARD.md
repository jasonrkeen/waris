## Intended use and inputs

Research demonstration of region/sector prioritisation and source separation. The supported runnable path is offline_demo.py with seeded synthetic inputs, not empirical wartime AML measurement. Generated regional/sector patterns can reflect the generator's assumptions. No institutional endorsement, operational effectiveness or predictive validity is claimed.

Seven inherited risk layers use fixed configured weights. Revenue/plausibility components include sample-relative transformations; rankings can change with the reference population. Thresholds, weights and Monte Carlo perturbation distributions are not calibrated against verified AML outcomes. Simulation frequencies are assumed-model sensitivity results, not empirical violation probabilities or calibrated confidence intervals.
