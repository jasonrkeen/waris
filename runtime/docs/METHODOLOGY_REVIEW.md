# Confidence and ACLED policy — controlled methodology review

This is an unpublished continuation of frozen rc12, packaged separately as rc13. It is an engineering/documentation policy, not independent scientific validation or provider permission. The machine-readable METHODOLOGY_POLICY.json was frozen before the new retained-sample diagnostics. Model version 0.12.0, synthetic scoring formulas/weights, seed42 and 5000 Monte Carlo draws remain unchanged. GRP is benchmark-only and unverified FIU annual-report claims remain excluded.

Confidence labels are first-party qualitative declarations. High, Medium-High, Medium, Medium-Low, Low and Unknown are accepted exactly. Medium-High is preserved rather than assigned an invented numeric midpoint. Unknown is a separate category, not the lowest score and not a value to average. The assessment reports label counts per model-area/source-type group and the number of Unknown entries. Counts sum to field_count. Missing/unsupported labels, missing grouping keys, duplicated field identities and unsupported replacement-priority labels stop processing. No average_confidence, probability, interval scale or measured reliability is produced. Highest declared High/Medium/Low replacement urgency remains a categorical planning choice separate from confidence. Recognising a declaration does not establish its factual accuracy, source rights, freshness or calibration.

The current register no longer describes ACLED as live or blended into production inputs. Both ACLED-related declarations are Unknown. Other inherited confidence labels remain declarations, without retroactive validation. Old numeric assessments remain excluded unchanged; they are not relabelled as if this policy had historically executed. Reporting schemas retain the descriptive confidence_interpretation and replacement_priority_interpretation columns, while numeric average/rank columns are retired.

The ACLED event/fatality proxy and synthetic blend are retired in this candidate. The old 70/30 event/fatality weighting and 50/50 synthetic blend lacked demonstrated AML calibration; no replacement weights are chosen. The original synthetic-only demonstration continues, with its limitations intact. ACLED acquisition and the live entry point stay gated. A direct call to the retired proxy fails; a supplied nonempty ACLED table is refused by scoring enrichment. Reviewing data privately through src.acled_context does not clear it for scoring or redistribution.

The separate inspector describes a supplied retained sample using exact normalized explicit aliases, not address substrings. Kyiv City stays distinct from Kyiv oblast. It retains all model regions: a region with no sample record has null event/fatality fields and not_observed_in_retained_sample status. It does not mean zero conflict, zero source events or low risk. Out-of-universe/unmapped rows are counted separately, never silently reassigned. A fingerprint identifies retained bytes but is not an HTTP acquisition receipt. Region presence is not a population-coverage rate or sampling probability.

Source zero reported fatalities remain zero with a qualification. [ACLED's fatality methodology](https://acleddata.com/methodology/fatalities) says the field contains reported estimates; source zero can include missing/unknown fatality information. The original selected fields lack the notes needed to distinguish those cases, so the inspector cannot recover a latent true death count or reclassify source zeros. Blank/null fatality cells remain missing. A partially observed region gets a labelled partial known sum, a missing-value count and a null complete sum. Nonnumeric nonmissing, negative or nonfinite values are rejected. Duplicate/blank event IDs, invalid dates, country/type violations and date/year contradictions also stop processing. Dates describe recorded events, not acquisition time or independently verified day precision.

A page cap is not a sampling design. [ACLED's API documentation](https://acleddata.com/api-documentation/acled-endpoint) exposes filters and a with_total option, but the retained historical request/total/page receipts are missing. The source code recipe and a row count equal to its limit do not establish the exact query, complete frame or random/representative selection. No source rate, missing-event estimate or complete-national-coverage claim is inferred. The model universe itself is narrower than all possible source geographies.

Before any future scoring proposal: authenticate acquisition/vintage and freeze a study period, source scope, mapping and population/frame; resolve provider and output-specific rights; document source zero/missingness and coverage limitations; define the intended AML-related construct and causal/measurement rationale; pre-specify candidate transformations, weights and sensitivity tests; obtain suitable independent review and lawful outcome evidence. Source completeness alone would not establish completeness of real-world reporting. No such future study is executed or authorised by these notes, and new downloads cannot authenticate the historical file.

This candidate bundles source, tests and policy documents only. Retained provider events, regional aggregates, context tables and corrected historical scores are not bundled. The private review receipts describe local engineering checks. Passing tests and a clean ZIP do not approve the full WARIS research release.
