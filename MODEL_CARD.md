# WARIS controlled research model card

Model 0.12.0; current code baseline 0.12.0-rc13-methodology-review. The candidate identity is [RELEASE_SCOPE.json](RELEASE_SCOPE.json), separate from model and presentation identities. This is the full selected research package: a runnable synthetic architecture plus separately bounded research components, benchmarks and appendices.

## Intended use

Research, reproducible engineering review and inspection of source separation. The supported entry points are the controlled dashboard/report and the offline synthetic/research replays described in [README](README.md). Scores, region-sector rankings and 5,000-draw sensitivity summaries are wholly synthetic. Real place names do not make them empirical observations. Thresholds, layer weights and perturbation distributions have not been calibrated to verified AML outcomes; simulated frequencies and p05/p95 ranges are not observed violation probabilities or empirical confidence intervals.

## Source roles

Official 2021 GRP stays benchmark-only: no scoring, Monte Carlo integration or validated sector-economic allocation. Procurement is recorded-frame descriptive research, with procedure, lifecycle, lot, sampling and missing-bid limits; object counts are not unique firms, misconduct or national prevalence. World Bank and selected verified SFMS facts are national context only. Quarterly SFMS verification was text-only without local PDF custody; it does not authenticate the missing 2023 annual report. Unverified FIU claims remain excluded. Six literature themes are conceptual reference; numerical mappings are project interpretations. Workflow/control counts are declarations, not completed analyst decisions or measured effectiveness.

The old ACLED intensity proxy and synthetic blend remain retired; raw retained events and acquisition history do not establish current wartime coverage or redistribution entitlement. Live preflight stays gated before credentials/provider access. Confidence remains categorical, including a separate Unknown category, with no numeric confidence average or confidence-adjusted score.

## Evidence and limits

The parent security-integrated package passed 405 tests plus three subtests on each of Windows and Linux WSL2, seven dashboard sections, offline replays and controlled builds. Those are dated parent engineering results. Exact checks of this documentation/distribution child are recorded separately in the adjacent audit; do not substitute the parent results for fresh execution or claim remote GitHub CI. [Platform policy](PLATFORM_REPRODUCIBILITY.md) requires same-platform byte repeatability and exact source/metric, PDF/table and chart-pixel equivalence across qualified platforms.

This package does not establish empirical AML validation, historical request authentication, independent SME approval, fairness/false-positive performance, source coverage, operational effectiveness or provider endorsement. Thirteen formerly held mixed artifacts remain excluded unchanged; 23 scoped eligible ledger entries represent 20 unique files and three aliases, not 23 independent findings.

## Prohibited uses and change control

Do not use this model for allegations about entities, automated adverse action, sanctions/enforcement targeting, credit denial or interpretation of disruption/missingness as wrongdoing. It is not an operational decision system. New source acquisition, live scoring, protected-layer integration or scientific claims require separate rights evidence, a frozen design, validation and methodological review.
